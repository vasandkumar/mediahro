# Sharing, wallpaper, print and copy

Open a photo and use the **⋮** menu at the top, or select photos and use **More**.

## Share

**Share** opens a sheet before the system share sheet:

- **Size:** original, or at most 2048, 1280 or 640 pixels on the long side. Smaller files send faster and take less data.
- **Remove location and camera info:** off to start with; switch it on for one share, or turn it on for every share in **Settings → Photos**. The picture itself is not recompressed for this (JPEG, PNG, WebP, JPEG XL): only the details leave. Orientation and colour profile stay so the photo looks the same. HEIC, AVIF and RAW photos are saved again as JPEG without the details.

Then the system share sheet opens, with everything your phone offers: messaging apps, Nearby Share / Quick Share, e-mail, cloud drives. Photos that are not plain files on the device (the phone's photo library, network shares) are copied to a temporary folder first; the copies are removed after a couple of hours.

## Set as wallpaper

Choose home screen, lock screen or both (what your device allows). Available on Android and on desktop computers.

## Print

Choose how many photos go on a page, whether they fill it, and whether the file name is printed under each; the system print dialog opens next.

## Copy image

Puts the photo on the clipboard to paste into another app that accepts pictures.
