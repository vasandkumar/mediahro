# Finding duplicates

**Albums → Duplicates** looks for two kinds:

- **Exact copies**: files with identical content (compared by a fingerprint of the file, not by name).
- **Look the same**: photos that look alike even when one is smaller, recompressed or slightly edited. Mediahro makes a tiny greyscale picture of each photo and compares them; the picture is remembered, so the next search is quick.

Nothing is deleted on its own. Tap a group to see its photos side by side with shared zoom and pan. The largest, best-quality one is kept to start with; turn **Keep** on or off for each photo and tap **Delete others**: those photos go to **Recently deleted**, where they stay for 30 days. You must keep at least one.

The search can take a while on a large library; **Stop** ends it and shows what was found so far.
