# Viewing a photo

Tap a photo to open it full screen.

## Moving around

- **Swipe** left or right for the next or previous photo, or tap a photo in the strip along the bottom.
- **Pinch** to zoom, drag to move around a zoomed photo, and **double-tap** to step through fit, 1:1 and closer.
- **Fit or fill**: the button at the bottom switches between showing the whole photo and filling the screen. **Rotate** turns the picture a quarter turn for viewing; it does not change the file.
- The **percentage** at the bottom shows the zoom. 100% means one pixel of the photo per pixel of your screen.
- **Tap** the picture to hide or show the controls. They hide by themselves after a few seconds (unless you use a screen reader or switch control).

## Very large photos

Pictures of 100 megapixels and more open quickly: Mediahro shows a smaller version first and loads sharp detail for the part you are looking at as you zoom in.

## Live Photos and Motion Photos

A Live Photo (an iPhone photo with a short **.MOV** beside it) or a Motion Photo (a short video stored inside the JPEG) shows **Live** or **Motion** on its thumbnail. In the viewer press the play button at the bottom to watch the clip once over the picture, with its sound at the player's volume. It stops by itself, when you press the button again, or when you swipe to another photo. Nothing is copied: the clip is played straight out of the photo's file (or the .MOV next to it).

## Panoramas and multi-page files

A **panorama** (a photo marked as one, at least about twice as wide as it is tall) opens filling the screen's height so you can drag it sideways along its length; the fit button shows the whole picture.

A **multi-page TIFF** (a scan, a fax) shows **Page 1 of 5** at the bottom with previous and next buttons.

## 360° photos

A photo that is marked as a 360° sphere (Google's Photo Sphere tags, or a whole sphere twice as wide as it is tall) has a **Look around in 360°** button at the bottom of the viewer. It opens the photo as if you stood inside it:

- **Drag** to look around; a quick flick keeps turning and slows down (not with reduced motion). **Pinch** or use the mouse wheel to zoom between 20° and 100°.
- The buttons at the bottom do the same without a gesture: look left, right, up and down, zoom in and out, and **Reset the view**. With a keyboard use the arrows, **+** and **−**, and **0** (or Home) to reset. A screen reader says it is a 360° photo, which way it faces, and offers the same moves as actions.
- Where the app can read the phone's motion sensors, the camera can follow the phone: turn around and the view turns with you, from wherever you were looking. Use the phone button to stop or to start it again. It is off to begin with when the system asks for reduced motion, and the sensors are released when you leave the screen or the app goes away.
- A **cropped panorama** (a sweep that covers only part of the sphere) can be looked around in too, but never past its edges.

The picture is drawn on the graphics chip from the photo at up to 8192 pixels wide; nothing is uploaded.

## Camera RAW photos

A RAW photo is shown from the full-size preview your camera stored in the file. A note on screen says so.

## With a keyboard

Left and right arrows change photo, **+** and **−** zoom, **0** fits the photo, **1** shows it at 100%, **F** switches fit and fill, **R** rotates, **I** opens the details and **Esc** goes back.
