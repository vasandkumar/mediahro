# Viewing a photo

Tap a photo to open it full screen.

## Moving around

- **Swipe** left or right for the next or previous photo, or tap a photo in the strip along the bottom.
- **Pinch** to zoom, drag to move around a zoomed photo, and **double-tap** to step through fit, 1:1 and closer.
- **Fit or fill**: the button at the bottom switches between showing the whole photo and filling the screen. **Rotate** turns the picture a quarter turn for viewing; it does not change the file.
- The **percentage** at the bottom shows the zoom. 100% means one pixel of the photo per pixel of your screen.
- **Tap** the picture to hide or show the controls. They hide by themselves after a few seconds (unless you use a screen reader or switch control).

## Very large photos

Pictures of 100 megapixels and more open quickly: Mediahro shows a smaller version first and loads sharp detail for the part you are looking at as you zoom in.

## Camera RAW photos

A RAW photo is shown from the full-size preview your camera stored in the file. A note on screen says so.

## With a keyboard

Left and right arrows change photo, **+** and **−** zoom, **0** fits the photo, **1** shows it at 100%, **F** switches fit and fill, **R** rotates, **I** opens the details and **Esc** goes back.
