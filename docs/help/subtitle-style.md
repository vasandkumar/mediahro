# How subtitles look and when they appear

## Style

**Settings → Subtitles** sets the size, text colour, outline, background box, font, position and alignment. Each choice starts at **System**, which follows your device's caption style (the accessibility caption settings on Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows) where the device has one, including changes you make there while a video plays. If you turned captions on in those settings, videos start with subtitles in your language. Changes apply to the playing video at once.

Styled subtitles (ASS and SSA) keep their own look, such as karaoke colours and positions. To make them use your settings too, turn on **Use these settings for styled subtitles**.

## Timing

- **Delay:** in the player, **Subtitles → Subtitle delay** moves subtitles earlier or later in 50 ms steps, up to 60 seconds either way. On a keyboard, Z and X do the same; on a phone, swipe left or right with two fingers. The delay is remembered for each video.
- **Speed:** subtitles made for a video with a different frame rate drift further off as the video plays. **Subtitles → Subtitle speed** converts between common frame rates, for example **From 23.976 to 25 fps**. The choice is remembered for each video.

## Two subtitles at once

**Subtitles → Second subtitles** shows another track at the same time, for example to learn a language. It has its own delay. In **Settings → Subtitles** you can set its size and choose whether it sits at the top of the picture or just above the first subtitles.
