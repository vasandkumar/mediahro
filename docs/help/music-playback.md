# Music playback

## Formats

Mediahro plays MP3, AAC and M4A, FLAC, ALAC, WAV and AIFF (up to 32-bit, 768 kHz), Opus, Vorbis, WavPack, APE, TTA, TAK, MPC, DSD files (DSF and DFF), WMA, surround audio files and tracker modules (MOD, XM, IT, S3M).

## Gapless playback

Albums play without gaps between tracks, so live albums and continuous mixes sound as intended.

## Crossfade

Crossfade blends the end of one track into the start of the next for up to 12 seconds. You can set different fades for when a track ends and for when you skip. Crossfade is off for gapless albums unless you turn it on for them.

## Volume levelling

Mediahro reads ReplayGain tags to play tracks or albums at the same loudness. Tracks without tags are measured in the background. You can choose the loudness target, add a pre-amp and turn on clipping prevention.

## Equaliser and effects

- 10-band graphic equaliser and a parametric equaliser with up to 32 bands, with presets
- Import headphone correction presets (AutoEq for APO or Wavelet)
- Limiter, balance, mono, channel mixer and crossfeed for headphones
- Change tempo and pitch independently
