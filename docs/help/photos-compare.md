# Comparing photos

Use compare to choose the best of a burst or a few similar shots.

## Starting

- **From the viewer:** tap **Add to compare** (the two-panel icon at the top). Add up to four photos, swiping between them; when two or more are waiting, the message that appears has a **Compare now** button.
- **From a selection:** select 2 to 4 photos, then **More → Compare**.

Videos cannot be compared.

## Looking

- **Side by side** (two, three or four photos): all photos pan and zoom together, so the same spot of each is always in view. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag to move; **Reset zoom** puts the view back.
- With two photos you can also use **Swipe** (drag the divider; arrow keys move it too), **Overlay** (the second photo faded over the first with a slider) and **Difference** (shows where the two photos differ; black means identical).

## Keeping and rejecting

Under the photos there is a card for each one with three buttons:

- **Keep**: marks the photo as a favourite.
- **Reject**: marks it rejected. Rejected photos stay where they are and are collected in **Albums → Rejected**; nothing is deleted. A rejection replaces a star rating, and a favourite loses its heart.
- **Keep only this one**: keeps this photo and rejects the others in the comparison.

Tap a button again to undo it. Decisions are saved as you make them. With **Settings → Photos → Write ratings, labels and tags into photo files** on, a rejection is also saved in the file as the standard XMP "rejected" rating (-1).
