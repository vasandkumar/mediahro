# Playlists, smart playlists, widgets and the car

## Playlists

The **Playlists** tab on the Music screen lists your playlists.

- **New playlist** makes an empty one. Add songs from any song's menu (**Add to playlist**) or from Now Playing. Drag the handle to reorder songs, and swipe a song to remove it.
- Open the menu (⋮) in a playlist to rename or delete it.
- **Save as playlist** in the queue turns the current queue into a playlist.

## Smart playlists

A smart playlist fills itself from rules and is always up to date. Tap **New smart playlist**, name it and add conditions:

- **What you can use:** title, artist, album artist, album, genre, composer, year, track and disc number, play count, last played, rating, date added, length (in minutes), file path, folder, file type, codec, and your tags (including Favourites).
- **How to match:** each condition has an operator suited to the field, for example *contains*, *is*, *is at least*, *is between*, or *in the last … days*.
- **Combining conditions:** **Match all** needs every condition, **Match any** just one. **Add group** adds a sub-group with its own all/any, for example "Jazz, and either rated highly or played this week".
- **Limit:** a number of songs, minutes or MB.
- **Sort by:** any field, or **Random**.

While you edit, the count of matching songs updates live. To change a smart playlist later, open it and tap the rule button.

## Widgets and quick controls

The home-screen widget (Android), the Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets (iPhone and iPad), the menu-bar control (Mac) and the taskbar thumbnail buttons and media keys (Windows) show the playing song and its cover, with play/pause and skip. They change as soon as the song or the play state changes.

## In the car

With Android Auto or CarPlay, Mediahro offers your **Queue**, **Playlists**, **Albums**, **Artists** and **Folders**. Choosing a song plays its album, playlist or folder from that song. You can also search, or ask by voice, for example "play So What by Miles Davis".
