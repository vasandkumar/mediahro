# Slideshow

Start a slideshow from the **slideshow button** at the bottom of the viewer (it plays from the photo you are on, through the photos you were looking at), from the slideshow button at the top of an album or Favourites, or from **More → Slideshow** on the photos you selected. Videos are left out.

- **Tap** the screen to show or hide the controls. They hide by themselves after a few seconds (unless you use a screen reader or switch control).
- **Play / pause**, **previous** and **next** photo; the arrow keys and the space bar do the same.
- **Speed:** the rewind and fast-forward buttons slow down or speed up the show by a quarter step each time, from ¼× to 4×, while it runs (also **+** and **−**). How long a photo stays is the interval you chose divided by the speed.
- **Options** (the sliders button): seconds per photo (2 to 60), the transition (none, fade, slide, zoom), **Slow zoom (Ken Burns)**, **Random order**, **Loop**, and **Music**. Your choices are remembered for next time.
- **Random order** shows every photo once, starting at the one you began on, before repeating (if Loop is on).
- Without **Loop** the show stops at the last photo with **Play again**.
- **Music** plays from your Music library: the whole library shuffled, or a playlist you choose. It starts with the slideshow and pauses when you leave it.
- The screen stays awake during the show. If your phone asks for reduced motion, the zoom and the sliding transitions are replaced by a short fade.
- A photo that cannot be opened is skipped.

## External displays

A phone or tablet that mirrors its screen to a TV or monitor shows the slideshow there like anything else. Showing the slideshow on the second screen on its own, while the phone shows controls, is not available yet.
