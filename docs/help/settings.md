# Settings

Open **Settings** from the bottom bar on phones or the sidebar on tablets and computers. On a computer you can also press Ctrl+, (⌘, on a Mac).

## Find an option

Type in **Search settings** at the top. Search covers option names, their descriptions and related words: "night" finds **Theme**, for example. Every option has a one-line description of what it does.

Options grouped by section: Playback, Video, Audio, Subtitles, Photos, Library, Sources, Appearance, Keyboard shortcuts, Privacy, Support Mediahro and About. Rarely needed options sit in a collapsed **Show advanced options** group at the end of a section.

## Appearance

- **Theme:** light, dark, or follow your device.
- **Pure black:** black backgrounds in the dark theme. This saves power on OLED screens.
- **Higher contrast:** stronger text and outline colours. "Follow system" uses your device's contrast setting.
- **Colours from wallpaper** (Android 12 and later): use your wallpaper's colours instead of each mode's own colour.
- **Colour per mode:** Video, Music and Photos each use their own colour. Turn it off to use one colour everywhere. Under **Show advanced options** you can pick another colour for each mode. Colours are adjusted automatically so text stays readable.
- **Language:** the language of menus and messages. "Follow system" uses your device's language. Arabic and Hebrew lay the app out from right to left.
- **Open where you left off:** start in Video, Music or Photos, whichever you used last.
- **Reduce motion** and **Reduce transparency** (advanced): use fades instead of moving animations, and solid bars and sheets instead of see-through ones. Both also follow your device's accessibility settings.

## Reset a section

At the end of each section, **Reset section** returns that section's options to their defaults after you confirm. Your library, playlists and sources are not affected.

## Move settings to another device

In the **More actions** menu (⋮ next to the title):

- **Export settings** saves your settings to a file or copies them as text. Sign-in details are never included, and neither is which mode you used last.
- **Import settings** applies settings pasted from an export. They replace your current settings. Values from a newer version of Mediahro are refused with a message.
- **Reset all settings** returns every option to its default.

## Options managed by your organisation

On devices managed by a company or school, some options show a lock and "Managed by your organisation". Your organisation sets these, and they can't be changed in the app. Imports skip them.
