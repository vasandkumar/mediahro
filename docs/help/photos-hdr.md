# HDR photos

Photos with an HDR gain map (Ultra HDR JPEG, HDR HEIC and AVIF) and HDR JPEG XL or AVIF files carry more brightness and colour than a normal screen shows. Mediahro shows them with their full range when your screen supports HDR, and as a normal picture otherwise.

- In the timeline an HDR photo has an **HDR** badge.
- Open the photo. On an HDR screen the system's HDR picture is shown while the photo is at rest at its fit size. Zooming, turning or filling the screen switches to the regular zoomable picture, which is tone-mapped to look right on any screen; zooming back out shows the HDR picture again. Swiping moves between photos as usual.
- The **HDR** button in the tool row turns the HDR picture off and on for the photo you are looking at (it starts on).
- On a screen without HDR nothing changes: every photo is shown tone-mapped, and the button is not offered.
- Editing, comparing, thumbnails and sharing use the tone-mapped picture; the original file keeps its HDR data unless you replace it with an edit.
