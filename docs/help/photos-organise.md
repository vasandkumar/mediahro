# Organising photos

## Selecting

Press and hold a photo to start selecting. Tap more photos to add or remove them, or keep your finger down and drag across photos to select a whole run (drag across selected photos to deselect them). **Select all** selects everything in the view you are in. The bar at the top shows what you can do with the selection.

## Favourites, ratings and labels

- **Heart**: marks the selected photos as favourites. Favourites show a small heart on their tile and are collected under **Albums → Favourites**.
- **Rate**: 1 to 5 stars, or no rating.
- **Colour label**: one of seven colours.

If a photo already carries a rating, a colour label or keywords in its own file (other photo programs write them there), Mediahro picks them up the first time it sees the photo. After that, changes you make in Mediahro stay in Mediahro.

## Tags

**Tags** adds your own words to the selected photos and removes them again. Tags are searchable.

## Albums

**Add to album** puts the selected photos into an album you choose, or into a new one. Open an album from the **Albums** tab; inside it, **Remove from album** takes photos out of the album without deleting them. Deleting an album never deletes its photos.

### Smart albums

A smart album collects photos by rules, for example "rating is at least 4 stars and favourite" or "taken in the last 30 days". It updates by itself as your photos change. Create one from **New album → New smart album**.

## Folders

The **Folders** tab shows your photos the way they are stored: each source, its folders and the photos inside. Smaller or larger photos, and square or proportional rows, work here as everywhere.
