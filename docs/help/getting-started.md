# Getting started

Mediahro opens without an account or sign-in. The first screen offers three actions:

- **Open a file** plays or shows a single file.
- **Add a folder** adds a folder to your library. Mediahro asks for access only when you choose this.
- **Add a server** connects a network share or a media server such as Jellyfin.

Switch between **Video**, **Music** and **Photos** with the bottom bar on phones or the sidebar on tablets and computers.

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on a feature that needs the internet. See Settings → Privacy.
