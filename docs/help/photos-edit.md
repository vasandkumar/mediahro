# Editing photos

Open a photo and tap **Edit photo** (the sliders icon at the top). The original file is not touched until you save. What you have not saved is kept: close the editor and open the photo again to carry on.

## Tools

- **Crop** — drag the corners or edges of the box, or its middle to move it; pick a shape (**Free**, **Original**, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9 and the upright versions). **Turn left / right** and **Flip** change the picture's direction. **Straighten** tilts it by up to 45° and trims the edges so no empty corners show. The arrow keys nudge the crop box; Shift and an arrow resizes it.
- **Light** — exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks.
- **Colour** — temperature, tint, saturation, vibrance.
- **Detail** — sharpen, and a vignette (darker corners, or lighter with a negative value).
- **Curves** — RGB and one curve per colour. Tap to add a point, drag it to move it, drag it out of the box to remove it. Every point can also be moved with the accessibility actions or **Reset curve**.
- **Draw** — pen, line, arrow, box, in eight colours and three widths, and **Blur** to hide a face or a number plate. **Remove drawings and blurs** clears them.

Double-tap the name of a slider to put it back to zero. **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes; one drag of a slider is one step. **More → Reset all edits** starts again (that too can be undone).

## Saving

- **Save a copy** writes the edited photo next to the original as `name_edited.jpg` (`_edited_2`, … if that exists). A PNG, WebP or JPEG XL stays in its own format; HEIC, AVIF, RAW, TIFF and the like are saved as JPEG. Camera information, location and date are carried over.
- **Replace the original** overwrites the file after a warning. It is offered for JPEG, PNG, WebP and JPEG XL only; a JPEG is saved again, which loses a little quality. Copies are the safe way.

Large photos are edited at full size when you save; while you edit, a reduced copy keeps the preview quick.

## Same look on many photos

In the editor choose **More → Copy this look**. Then select photos and use **More → Apply copied look** to give them the light, colour, detail and curves (not the crop, straightening or drawings). You can save copies or replace the originals.
