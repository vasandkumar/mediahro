# Now Playing, sound, sleep timer and alarms

## Now Playing controls

Under the transport controls in Now Playing:

- **Favourite** (heart) adds the song to your Favourites tag, so smart playlists and search can find it.
- **Rating** (star) opens a five-star rating. Tap a full star again to give half a star.
- **Sound** opens the equalizer, volume levelling and crossfade (see below).
- **Output** shows how the audio reaches your device: device, sample rate, bit depth, whether the system resamples it, and the Bluetooth codec when there is one. You can also pick another output device where the system offers one.
- **Sleep timer** (moon) stops playback later; see below.
- **Add to playlist** adds the song to one of your playlists or a new one.
- **Song info** shows the file, format, size, length, play count and ReplayGain values.

## Sound

Open **Sound** from Now Playing, or from the menu on the Music screen.

- **Equalizer:** turn it on and pick a preset (Flat, Bass boost, Treble boost, Vocal, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Electronic, Loudness), or drag the ten bands and the pre-amp yourself. **Save as preset** keeps your curve; tap the × on a saved preset to delete it.
- **Volume levelling:** off, ReplayGain per track, ReplayGain per album, or measured loudness (EBU R128) for songs without ReplayGain tags.
- **Crossfade:** 0–12 seconds between songs. At 0, songs play gaplessly. Albums meant to play without gaps are never crossfaded. **Also when skipping** crossfades manual skips too.

## Sleep timer

Choose 5–90 minutes, **End of this song**, or after 2, 3 or 5 songs. The music fades out over the last 30 seconds, then pauses; the next time you press play, the volume is back to normal. While a timer runs, the moon is filled in and its label says when playback stops. Open it again to **Add 5 minutes** or **Turn off timer**.

## Alarms

Wake up to your music. Open **Alarms** from the menu on the Music screen and tap **Add alarm**:

- set the time, the days to repeat (none means once), and a label
- choose a playlist, or the current queue
- optionally shuffle

The music fades in when the alarm goes off. A one-off alarm switches itself off afterwards. Alarms need the system's alarm support. Where a device doesn't offer it yet, the Alarms screen says so.

## Interruptions

Music pauses for phone calls and when another app starts playing, and resumes afterwards when the system allows it. It turns down briefly for notifications, and pauses when you unplug headphones or a Bluetooth device disconnects.
