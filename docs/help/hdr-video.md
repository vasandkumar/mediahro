# HDR video

Mediahro draws video on your device's graphics processor, so even 4K and 10-bit video plays smoothly.

## HDR10 and HLG

HDR videos (HDR10 and HLG) are converted for your screen so they keep their contrast and colour and never look grey or washed out. The default conversion adapts to each scene. You can choose another tone-mapping curve in the video settings: spline, Reinhard, Hable, Möbius or clip.

## HDR with dynamic metadata

Videos that carry dynamic HDR metadata play with correct colours. When your device cannot use the dynamic metadata, Mediahro uses the video's standard HDR10 information.

Some dynamic HDR videos contain an extra enhancement layer. Mediahro plays the main picture and shows a one-time note that the extra layer is not used. Playback never shows a black screen.

The **Info** sheet in the player (or **I** on a keyboard) shows the video's HDR format and peak brightness.
