# Converting videos for casting

Some TVs and speakers cannot play every video format. When you cast such a video, Mediahro converts it on your device while it plays and sends the converted stream to the TV. It uses your device's hardware video encoder when there is one.

Conversion happens only while you cast. Mediahro never changes or replaces the files in your library.

Converting uses more battery and processing power than casting a file directly, so keep a phone or tablet plugged in for long videos.
