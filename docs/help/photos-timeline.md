# The photo timeline

Photos shows every picture and video from your sources, newest first, grouped by the day they were taken (or by month when the photos are small).

## Finding your way

- **Scroll fast with the edge handle.** Drag the handle on the right edge to jump to a month; a bubble shows where you are.
- **Change the size of the photos.** Pinch with two fingers, or open the size button in the top bar and choose 1 to 6 columns.
- **Square or proportional.** The layout button switches between a tidy square grid and rows that keep each photo's proportions.

## What the little labels mean

- **RAW**: a camera RAW file.
- **Live**: an iPhone Live Photo (the picture and its short video).
- **Motion**: a Motion Photo (a picture with a few seconds of video inside).
- **HDR**: a picture with extra brightness range for HDR screens.
- A time such as **1:23** marks a video.

## Speed

Mediahro makes small preview pictures of your photos in the background, starting with the ones you can see and then the ones near them. They are kept on the device so the next visit is instant. If previews ever take too much space, clear them in Settings; they are made again as you scroll.

Pictures from a network share or cloud drive are read only when needed. Nothing in your photos is uploaded anywhere.
