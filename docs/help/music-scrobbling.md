# Scrobbling

Scrobbling shares the songs you listen to with ListenBrainz, so you can keep a listening history and get recommendations there. It is off until you connect a service.

Open **Music → More (⋮) → Scrobbling**.

## Connecting a service

- **ListenBrainz:** copy your user token from the settings page of your ListenBrainz account and paste it in. The token is kept in your device's secure storage.

Before connecting, Mediahro tells you what is sent: the title, artist, album and time of each song you play. Connecting turns on ListenBrainz in **Settings → Privacy**; disconnecting turns it off again.

## When a song counts

A song is scrobbled once it has played for half its length or four minutes, whichever comes first. Songs shorter than 30 seconds are never scrobbled. Seeking forward does not count as listening. Radio stations are not scrobbled.

## Offline

Listens wait on your device until they can be sent, also across restarts. **Send now** tries straight away; otherwise Mediahro tries again later. If a service signs you out (for example after you change your password), sending stops and the screen asks you to connect again; waiting listens are kept.

## Leaving sources out

Under **Don't scrobble from**, switch off any library source whose songs you don't want to share.
