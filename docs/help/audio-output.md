# Audio output and quality

## What you are hearing

The output quality indicator shows the sample rate and bit depth your device is actually playing, and whether the sound reaches the device unchanged (**bit-perfect**), converted to another rate (**resampled**) or mixed with other sounds (**mixed**).

## Bit-perfect playback

Turn on bit-perfect output in the audio settings to send music to your DAC unchanged. The device switches to each track's sample rate automatically.

- **Android 14 and later:** bit-perfect output to USB DACs.
- **Mac:** exclusive access to the output device.
- **iPhone and iPad:** the closest match the system allows.
- **Windows:** exclusive mode (WASAPI).

Effects such as the equaliser, volume levelling and crossfade change the sound, so the indicator shows when they are active.

## DSD

DSD files are converted to high-resolution PCM. You can choose the conversion filter in the audio settings.

## Bluetooth

Mediahro shows the Bluetooth codec in use and avoids cutting off the first seconds of a track when a headset wakes up. For videos, set an audio delay in the audio settings if sound and picture are out of sync over Bluetooth.

## Calls and other apps

Mediahro lowers the volume or pauses for calls, notifications and other apps, and pauses when you unplug headphones. Choose when playback resumes in the audio settings.
