# Audiobooks

Mediahro plays audiobooks as books, not as a pile of songs: it remembers where you are in each one, and its speed.

## What counts as a book

- An **M4B** file.
- A folder of files (MP3, M4A and others) whose tags say **Audiobook** or **Spoken Word** as the genre. The files play in track order.
- Anything you mark yourself. In **Folders**, open a folder's **⋮** menu and choose **Treat as audiobook**. On a single song, use its **⋮** menu. A book you'd rather have as music: open its menu in **Audiobooks** and choose **Treat as music**.

Books are in the **Audiobooks** tab. The ones you are listening to come first, then the rest, finished ones last. Each shows how much is left. Tap a book to carry on where you stopped.

## Chapters

Mediahro reads chapters from MP4/M4B chapter marks, MP3 chapter frames (ID3 CHAP) and OverDrive markers. A CUE sheet next to a single file (same name, `.cue`) works too. In a folder of files, each file is a chapter. Use the chapter list (**≡**) to jump, or the previous and next chapter buttons. Previous chapter restarts the chapter if you are a few seconds into it.

## While you listen

- **Back and forward 30 seconds.**
- **Speed**, 0.5× to 3×. Each book remembers its own; a new book starts at the speed you used last. Pitch is kept natural.
- **Skip silence** shortens long pauses. It is remembered per book too.
- **Bookmarks** (the bookmark button): mark the place with an optional note. Open the list to jump back, edit a note or delete one.
- **Sleep timer**: the usual timers, plus **End of this chapter**. Shake the phone while a timer runs to add five minutes (or one more track). Shaking is checked only while a timer runs.

## Good to know

- Your place is saved every few seconds and whenever you pause, so a call or a crash loses almost nothing.
- Finishing a book marks it finished. Opening it again starts from the beginning, or use **Start over** in its menu.
- Playing music while a book is open ends book mode: the book's place is saved and the speed goes back to normal.
