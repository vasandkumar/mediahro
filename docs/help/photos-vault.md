# The vault

The vault keeps private photos **encrypted** behind your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Open it from **Albums → Vault**.

- **Set up:** the first time, **Set up the vault** makes a random key and locks it with your device's secure hardware. You need a screen lock, fingerprint or face unlock on the device; without one the vault is not offered.
- **Move photos in:** in the viewer's ⋮ menu or **More** on a selection, choose **Move to vault**. The photo is encrypted, then removed from its folder, the timeline, search and other apps. If the original cannot be removed (a read-only source) it is not moved and stays where it was. Videos cannot go in yet.
- **Add from the vault:** the plus button picks photos from anywhere and encrypts them straight in.
- **Look:** tap a photo to view it; swipe for the next one. Pictures are decoded in memory only; nothing readable is written to disk and nothing from the vault shows in the timeline, the map, search or the recent list.
- **Take out:** select photos, **Take out**, and choose where to save them; they leave the vault when the copy is saved. **Delete for good** erases them without a trash.
- **Locking:** the vault locks when you press **Lock**, leave the screen, or send the app to the background.
- **Backups:** the vault folder is excluded from Android backups and from cloud sync. Its key lives in secure hardware on this device only: if you reset the device's lock method or restore to a new phone, the vault cannot be opened. Take photos out first if you plan to do either.

## Decoy vault

In the open vault, **⋮ → Decoy vault** makes a second, separate vault that opens with a PIN (4 to 12 digits) through **Use PIN** on the lock screen. It looks the same as the real one and holds only what you put in it, so you can show it if you are made to open the vault. **Use PIN** only appears once a decoy exists, and inside the decoy there is no menu that mentions it. Removing the decoy deletes everything in it.
