# Mediahro trademark policy

The code is AGPL-3.0-or-later. **The name and logo are not licensed by the AGPL.** This policy is based on the Model Trademark Guidelines and Mozilla's trademark policy.

## Marks
The word mark "Mediahro", the Mediahro logo, and their combination are owned by the Mediahro project entity. Use ™ until registration, ® afterwards.

## Allowed without asking
- Referring to the app by name in articles, reviews, tutorials, meetups and academic work.
- "Works with Mediahro", "plugin for Mediahro", "client for Mediahro".
- Linking to official downloads.
- Redistributing **unmodified** official builds with attribution.
- Package repositories (F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, Homebrew, winget, AUR, Flathub, Debian) using the name for unmodified or lightly patched builds.

## Allowed with conditions
Forks and modified builds must either remove the name and logo, **or** show "Unofficial build — not endorsed by the Mediahro project" prominently in the app name, store listing and About screen.

## Not allowed
- Publishing modified builds under the Mediahro name on any app store.
- Domains, handles or product names that begin with or contain "mediahro" (for example "MediahroTube", "Mediahro Pro").
- Merchandise; implying endorsement; altering the logo.
- Using the marks for IPTV, piracy-adjacent or content-directory products.

## Enforcement
Write to `legal@mediahro.com`. We resolve things in good faith first; impersonating store listings are reported to the store.
